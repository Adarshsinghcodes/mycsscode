The Problem

90% of Travelers Arrive in a New City Confused

First-time visitors face immediate disorientation, inflated pricing, and fractured tools.

Zero Local Knowledge

Travelers struggle to locate hygienic, affordable meals, identify safe neighborhoods, or avoid tourist and patient scams.

Disconnected App Sprawl

Google Maps gives raw location pins, Zomato covers food, and Oyo lists rooms—yet none connect the holistic user journey.

High Emergency Stress

Medical patients, family attendants, and student test-takers face peak emotional vulnerability without dependable on-ground support.

Core Insight: Travelers don't visit cities to browse generic pins—they travel with a dedicated, time-sensitive purpose.
